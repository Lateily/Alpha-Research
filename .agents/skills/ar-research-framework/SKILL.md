# AR Research Framework

1. Read `experiments/AGENTS.md` and the task's research authority documents.
2. Identify the producer, versioned contract, consumer, registration time,
   freshness rule, degraded state, and scoring path before changing logic.
3. Preserve point-in-time inputs and append-only corrections. Historical values
   may not be silently rewritten.
4. Missing evidence remains blocked or partial. Never substitute zero or stale
   data presented as current.
5. Separate constructive and cautious directions. Apply the independent causal
   cluster gate before any performance claim.
6. Keep calibration output advisory until its pre-registered promotion criteria
   and Junyan approval are satisfied.
7. Test one valid case, one missing/stale case, one malformed case, and the
   historical incident that motivated the change.
8. End research-facing output with the required non-instruction boundary.

The output is evidence and a falsifiable research record, not an action command.
