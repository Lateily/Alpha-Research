research workflow. It is intentionally under `experiments/` because Codex does
not own production protocol files.

screening + Bridge 8 paper/outcome tracking.

## Operating Principle

Do not upgrade any idea into an alpha claim until it has forward paper evidence.
The current proven edge is process discipline: record hypotheses before outcome,
score them after outcome, and distinguish flow facts from predictive claims.

Causal logic is valid because a pre-registered review loop reduces hindsight
bias and forces outcome attribution. Specific cadence choices, scoring rubrics,
and workflow thresholds in these documents are unvalidated intuitions unless
explicitly marked otherwise.

不是买卖指令；研究信号，human executes.
